## 01 A Hard Truth About Job Applications

The average time a recruiter spends reviewing a resume is **6 to 10 seconds**. Truly, just a quick glance.

In those 6 to 10 seconds, recruiters look for only one thing: **Does your experience match this specific job role?**

That means every resume you send out should be a tailored, customized version built for that exact position.

---

## 02 Why Is Rewriting Resumes Hard on Your Own?

Because not knowing what to write and knowing what you did but failing to structure it are two entirely different problems. You have accomplished a lot, but framing it into what the hiring manager wants to see requires:

1. **Understanding what the job requires** (Keyword Extraction)
2. **Finding matches in your past experience** (Alignment & Mapping)
3. **Translating into industry language** (Professional Rewriting)
4. **Keeping it to a single page** (Concise Editing)

These four steps happen to be what AI handles best.

## 04 Real Case Study

Our team tested this using a real team member background. The original resume was generic; we optimized it specifically for an **AI Platform Engineer** role.

### Optimization Highlights:

- **GPU Operations**: Changed Participated in GPU cluster ops -> Supported GPU cluster & hybrid-cloud inference environment operations.
- **Skill Abstraction**: Consolidated 3 disjointed CI/CD lines -> A structured platform engineering capability block.
- **Noise Reduction**: Stripped away legacy details irrelevant to Cloud/AI.
- **Open Source Relevance**: Added explicit links connecting open-source contributions to target job requirements.

**Result**: The candidate instantly looked tailored for the role. No fabrication--just letting AI reorganize real past accomplishments into a clear narrative.
